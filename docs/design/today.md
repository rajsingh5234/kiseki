# Today (decided 2026-11-22)

The home screen: what to study now, next and later, with progress beside it. Designs: [design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/NCpckrVSrxtNhVVmB2vWE1).

- **Phones (both sizes):** greeting, "Your plan for today", the Now card (its items, a ring for progress, the arrow to start), the Next card, then the rest of the day. The bottom bar stays.
- **Tablet portrait:** a side rail; the streak, done-today count and days to Prelims become a row of chips under the heading.
- **Tablet landscape, laptop, desktop:** the plan takes about 70% of the width; a progress column takes about 30% (Prelims countdown, done today, streak, hours in the last 7 days). Now and Next sit side by side on laptop and desktop, stacked on tablet landscape.
- **Ticking an item:** a bottom sheet on phones; a centred dialog on tablets and wider, with the same content (how clear it was, part done, add a note).
- **Streak and hours show the last 7 days**, so a Monday never looks empty; a day off appears as a dashed circle, never as a missed day.
