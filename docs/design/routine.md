# Routine (decided 2026-11-22)

The weekly routine: the user decides when (study and personal blocks for each day of the week); the app fills each study block with what to study. Used in setting up (step 5) and later from You.

- **Starting patterns** (setting up): Early start, Later start, Working (evenings on weekdays, longer weekends) and Start blank, each with a mini timeline; templates are data, so a track pack can suggest its own. Realistic by default (about 8 hours of study), since a routine that's too heavy makes every day end "behind".
- **The editor:** one day at a time on phones (a Mon–Sun strip, then that day's timeline); the whole week as seven columns on tablets and laptops. Each day shows its study total; days without study show "Day off". "Copy to…" lets the user pick exactly which days to copy to.
- **Editing a block:** tap a block (a sheet on phones, a dialog on wider screens): Study or Personal; for study, the track and a subject or "Planner picks" (the default: the app fills it with what's due); start and end in 15-minute steps with − / + buttons, or typed; "Repeat on" day chips; Delete and Save. Drag to move or resize comes on top as a shortcut, never the only way (WCAG 2.5.7).
- **Colours:** study blocks use their track or subject colour; personal blocks (work, meals, breaks) are neutral grey.
- **Wellbeing hints** (less than 7 hours for sleep, more than 3 hours without a break) appear in the editor as gentle notes with "Don't show again", as decided in the [daily journey](../product/phase-1/02-daily-journey.md).
