# Illustrations (decided 2026-11-21)

- **At key moments only** (about a dozen), never on daily study screens, which stay clean for focus:
  - **first-time setup:** welcome, picking a track, setting a routine;
  - **empty screens:** no tracks yet, no topics in a section, a day off, everything done today;
  - **earned milestones:** 7, 30 and 100-day streaks, a track's syllabus fully revised;
  - **friendly errors:** offline, something went wrong, page not found, the invite-only page.
- **Background patterns are not illustrations:** very faint shapes behind a card's content (sparkles, a large outline of the subject's symbol, a little lighter than the card) belong to the **surface style** ([themes, effects and motion](themes-effects-motion.md)), so daily screens may use them. They never carry meaning, are hidden from screen readers, keep text contrast at 4.5:1 over them, and are dropped when "increase contrast" is on, when patterns are switched off, or on a slow phone.
- **One consistent style:** soft, rounded, simple shapes, few colours; inspired by the UPSC user's favourite design but calmer and simpler, to suit a calm coach.
- **SVG coloured with theme tokens**, so every illustration recolours for every theme and for dark mode; each is checked in light and dark.
- **Small files**, loaded only on the screen that shows them.
- **Accessibility:** decorative illustrations are hidden from screen readers; one that carries meaning gets a text description.
- **Later:** gently animated illustrations through the motion engine, respecting reduce motion.
- **Source** (an open set with a clear licence, or an artist) is chosen at design time, with licences checked and recorded. AI-generated images are avoided unless their licence is clear, since copyright is uncertain and a consistent style is hard to keep.
