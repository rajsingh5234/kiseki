# Design references (2026-11-21)

She picked three published designs as references (inspiration only; Kiseki's screens are its own):

Fits Kiseki: "Your progress today" with a progress bar, a month calendar (her streak), list items with an arrow. Watch: light grey text on pastels must be darkened to pass contrast.
2. **Bright cards on white:** she likes its simple feel. Ideas to keep: a day strip, study cards with times, goals with "N days left" and progress, **one colour per subject**.
3. **Dark green glass:** a premium, calm dark look with frosted cards; a candidate for a dark theme family.
