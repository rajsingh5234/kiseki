# 002: Product name: Kiseki

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted

## Choice

The product is called **Kiseki** (Japanese 軌跡, "trajectory": the path you have travelled). It shows a learner's progress over months, step by step. It also sounds like 奇跡, "miracle".

In code, our own packages use the `@kiseki/` scope (e.g. `@kiseki/ui`, `@kiseki/contracts`). The repo folder is renamed from `study-planner` to `kiseki`.

## Alternatives

- **Kadam** (Hindi, "step"): several other brands use it, and Kadmik, a UPSC/SSC app, sounds close.
- **Jalon** (French, "milestone"): the most unique in study apps, but less personal.
- **Satat** (Hindi, "constant"): unique, and its `.app` domain is free.
- **Dhyey**, **Cursus**, **Kadmo**: names already used in our market or very close to existing apps.
- Common English words (Arc, Stride, Ember, Cadence…): used by many companies, hard to own.

## Why

- Not tied to UPSC or "planner", so it fits every track, extensions and themes.
- Short, easy to say, with a meaning that matches the product: a trajectory of progress.
- No study or productivity app uses the name (checked 2026-11-17).

## Risks and follow-ups

- **Kiteki**, a routine-planner app, sounds similar.
- *Kiseki* is also the name of a well-known video-game series, so plain searches may show games.
- `kiseki.com` (since 1999) and `kiseki.app` are taken. Choose a variant (e.g. `getkiseki.com`, `kiseki.study`) at the deploy step.
- Before launch: a trademark search on the official registry (India: ipindia.gov.in, software classes 9 and 42), then file our own trademark.
